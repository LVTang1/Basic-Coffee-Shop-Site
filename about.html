About Us

Firewalk is a dialogue driven story game centered around a researcher roaming the land of a fictional national park. You are tasked with uncovering the mystery behind the wildfires and keeping track of its regrowth. Our goal with this game is to educate people about the impacts of wildfires and the consequences that us humans have on nature.
