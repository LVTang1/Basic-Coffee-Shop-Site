About Us

Settled in the heart of Berkeley, our drinks are more than just a fresh brew- it's a cultural experience. From using authentic Vietnamese brewing methods, we bring the flavor of Vietnam to Berkeley. Filling the streets with the bright aroma of freshly brewed Coffee in the air, each cup is a taste of Asian culture. Along with a modern twist, we offer a wide variety of matcha that is simply unmatched. Our drinks are more than just a drink; they are a taste of home.
